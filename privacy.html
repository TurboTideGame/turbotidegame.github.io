Privacy Policy

Effective date: DRAFT for version 1.1 (not yet in effect)

The short version

TurboTide has no accounts. To find bugs and make the game better, it sends anonymous stats about how the game is played and reports when it crashes or freezes. They carry no name, email, account or advertising ID, and you can turn them off any time in Settings > SHARE STATS. Your progress is saved on your phone and backed up to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android) account, so it follows you to a new phone. The game shows video ads from Google AdMob: on iPhone, only when you choose to watch one to race certain courses; on Android, also a full-screen ad after a race. Google receives some information from your device to show and measure the ads, as explained below. On iPhone the game does not ask to track you. Buying the Full Game removes all ads.

Who we are

TurboTide is a racing game for iPhone and Android phones, made by Bungalow Studios LLC, an independent game studio in New York, USA. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Bungalow Studios LLC, the developer of TurboTide. "You" means the player.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in files on your phone so it can remember them:

  • Your progress, such as which courses and riders you have unlocked, the gold tokens you have found and the ad races you have used today.
  • Your best times, local high-score tables and a replay of your best Time Trial run on each course (the ghost you race against).
  • The initials you type for the high-score tables (3 letters).
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and hints.

We cannot see these files. If you delete the game, they are deleted from your phone with it; your cloud backup (below) stays in your own account until you remove it.

Cloud save (iCloud and Google backup)

So you don't lose your progress when you get a new phone or reinstall the game, it is backed up to your own cloud account:

  • On iPhone, the game keeps a copy of your progress in iCloud, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, if you are signed in to iCloud. Your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account get the same progress. The copy holds your progress, best times, initials and settings (not your purchases, which Apple restores itself).
  • On Android, your progress is included in Android's own backup to your Google Account, if backup is turned on in your phone's settings.

These copies are stored by Apple or Google in your account, under Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy. We never see them. To stop it, turn off iCloud for TurboTide in your iPhone's Settings (your name, then iCloud), or turn off backup in your Android phone's settings. To delete the copy, delete TurboTide's data in your iCloud storage settings, or your device's backup in your Google Account.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We never show ads during a race. On iPhone, ads play only when you choose to watch one. On Android, a full-screen ad can also play when you leave the results after a race.
  • Our stats and crash reports never include your name, email, contacts, Apple or Google Account, or advertising ID, and we never use them for ads or sell them.
  • On iPhone, the game does not ask for permission to track you across apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), so it never gets your device's advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell any information about you. Google's ad software in the game sends Google the information described below.

Game stats and crash reports

To find bugs, see which courses and features people enjoy, and make the game run better on more phones, the game sends us anonymous information while you play:

  • Game stats, sent to PostHog (a product analytics service run by PostHog, Inc., in the USA): which screens you open, races you start and finish (the mode, course, rider, difficulty, your place and time), tokens found, settings you change, purchases and ads started or finished (not payment details), and how smoothly the game ran (frame rate, load time, whether the phone got hot). Each stat comes with the game's version, the kind of phone (model and system version), its language and region setting, and a random ID the game makes when it is first opened.
  • Crash and freeze reports, sent to Sentry (an error reporting service run by Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), in the USA): when the game crashes, freezes or hits an error, a report with what the game was doing (the code that failed, the last few screens and races, the game's version), the kind of phone, its system version and memory, and the same random ID.

The random ID is made by the game, belongs only to TurboTide on that phone, and is not linked to you, your name, your Apple or Google Account or your advertising ID. Deleting the game deletes it. We ask both services not to keep your IP address: PostHog does not look up your location from it and discards it, and Sentry does not store it. Neither service builds a profile of you.

We use this information only to fix bugs and improve TurboTide. We do not use it for ads, do not combine it with other information about you, and do not sell or share it. We keep game stats for no longer than two years and crash reports for no longer than 90 days.

Your choice: turn off SHARE STATS in the game's Settings and the game stops sending both, at once. In the UK, Switzerland and the European Economic Area, SHARE STATS starts off and nothing is sent unless you turn it on. Test copies of the game send their stats and reports separately, so they never mix with yours.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Some courses can be raced by watching a short video ad. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, a service run by Google LLC, and never during a race. On iPhone, ads play only when you tap to watch one. On Android, the WATCH AD button works the same way, and a full-screen ad can also play after a race, as you leave the results.

To show an ad, measure it and fight fraud, Google's software in the game sends Google information from your device, such as your IP address (which gives a rough location), the device model and system version, the app's identifier, an identifier for this app on this device, when the app opens, how you interact with the ad, and how the app and Google's software are performing (for example, crash reports). The ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

On iPhone, the game does not ask to track you, so Google does not get your device's advertising identifier.

On Android, Google's software also gets your phone's advertising ID, an ID that Android gives apps for ads and that you can reset or delete, and the app set ID, an ID shared only by apps from the same developer on your phone. Google can use them to measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, fight fraud and, where the law and your choices allow it, show ads based on your interests. To reset or delete your advertising ID, open your phone's Settings, then Privacy (on some phones, Security and privacy, then More privacy settings), then Ads. Once it is deleted, apps, including this game, can no longer use it. On older versions of Android, the same screen has a switch to opt out of ads personalization instead.

Google handles this information under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information.

In the UK, Switzerland and the European Economic Area (and anywhere else the law requires it), Google asks for your consent before the first ad, on iPhone and on Android. You can change your choice any time in Settings > AD PRIVACY. If you decline, Google may still show limited ads that are not personalized. The Full Game turns off all ads.

Buying the Full Game turns off all ads for good, so no ad information is sent after that.

Game Center (iPhone)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game sends your Time Trial times to Game Center leaderboards, one for each course. Game Center is a service run by Apple. Your Game Center nickname and your times are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Apple handles this information under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account details. Game Center is optional: the game works the same without it, and you can sign out, or change what it shares, in your iPhone's Settings under Game Center.

The Android version has no online leaderboards: your times stay on your phone.

In-app purchase and codes

The game offers an optional, one-time in-app purchase, "Full Game: All Courses + No Ads", and may later offer more courses as separate packs. On iPhone, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. On Android, Google processes it through Google Play's billing system. We never see your name, payment card, Apple Account or Google Account details. Apple or Google tells the game only what has been bought, so it can open those courses, turn off ads and restore them on your other devices.

The Full Game can also be unlocked with a promotional code. On iPhone, codes are redeemed in the App Store, and Apple checks and redeems them. On Android, the game's Cheat Code screen opens Google Play's redeem page with the code filled in, and Google Play checks and redeems it. We do not learn who redeemed a code.

Apple's handling of purchases and codes is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's by Google's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

On iPhone, now and then, after a race, the game may ask iOS to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple shows and handles it, and decides how often it appears. Any rating or review you leave goes to the App Store, not to us. The Android version does not ask for ratings.

Vibration, notifications and permissions

The game uses vibration (haptics) for feedback. On iPhone, if you allow notifications, it can remind you about races with local notifications that your iPhone schedules itself; nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you can turn them off any time in Settings. The Android version does not send notifications. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or your phone's location services.

On Android, the game asks for no permissions you have to approve. It uses internet and network access (for game stats, crash reports, ads and Google Play), vibration and Google Play's billing, and Google's ad software adds permissions to read the advertising ID, use Android's ad services and run its own background tasks.

Children's privacy

TurboTide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous game stats and crash reports carry no name, contact details or advertising ID, and a parent can turn them off in Settings > SHARE STATS. The ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. A parent who wants no ads at all can buy the Full Game. On iPhone, a parent can also turn off in-app purchases and the game's network access in Screen Time; on Android, Google Play's parental controls and Google Family Link can require a parent's approval for purchases.

Keeping data safe

Game stats and crash reports go over encrypted connections and are kept by PostHog and Sentry on our behalf, for the times given above, and only we can see them. Your cloud save is kept by Apple or Google in your own account. What Google's software in the game sends for ads and purchases goes over encrypted connections, and Google keeps it as long as Google's Privacy Policy says.

Your privacy rights

Laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) give people rights to access, correct, delete or move their personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We respect these rights, and we sell nothing. Our game stats and crash reports are tied only to a random ID, not to you, so we usually cannot tell which ones are yours; you can stop them at any time with Settings > SHARE STATS, and deleting the game deletes the ID. If you want us to delete what was sent from your phone, email us and we will help. In the EU and UK we rely on your consent (SHARE STATS, which you turn on) for game stats and crash reports; elsewhere on our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the game, which you can object to by turning SHARE STATS off.

Information sent to Google for ads is held by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for your choices there. On iPhone, you can reset this app's identifier by deleting and reinstalling the game; on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (see Ads above). Your cloud save is in your iCloud or Google Account (see Cloud save above). Game Center data is held by Apple; manage it in your iPhone's Settings or at privacy.apple.com. Google Play purchases are held by Google; manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

If you have a question or want to confirm this, email us (see below). We will answer within 30 days.
