Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: DRAFT, takes effect with the first version of the game that shows ads

The short version

TurboTide has no accounts and no analytics, and your progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows optional video ads, only when you choose to watch one to race certain courses. Those ads come from Google AdMob, which receives some information from your device to show and measure them, as explained below. The game does not ask to track you, and buying the Full Game removes all ads.

Who we are

TurboTide is an iPhone racing game made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of TurboTide. "You" means the player.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in files on your iPhone so it can remember them:

  • Your progress, such as which courses and riders you have unlocked and the gold tokens you have found.
  • Your best times and local high-score tables.
  • The initials you type for the high-score tables (3 letters).
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and hints.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the game, this information is deleted with it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We never show ads you did not choose to watch, and never during a race.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • The game does not ask for permission to track you across apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), so it never gets your device's advertising identifier.
  • The game does not connect to any server of ours. The only things it sends go to Apple and, for ads, to Google, as described below.
  • We do not receive, sell or share any information about you.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Some courses can be raced by watching a short video ad. Ads play only when you tap to watch one. They are shown by Google AdMob, a service run by Google LLC.

To show an ad, measure it and fight fraud, Google's software in the game sends Google information from your device, such as your IP address (which gives a rough location), the device model and iOS version, the app's identifier, an identifier for this app on this device, and how you interact with the ad. Because the game does not ask to track you, Google does not get your device's advertising identifier. The ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Google handles this information under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information.

Buying the Full Game turns off all ads for good, so no ad information is sent after that.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game sends your Time Trial times to Game Center leaderboards, one for each course. Game Center is a service run by Apple. Your Game Center nickname and your times are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Apple handles this information under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account details. Game Center is optional: the game works the same without it, and you can sign out, or change what it shares, in your iPhone's Settings under Game Center.

In-app purchase and codes

The game offers an optional, one-time in-app purchase, "Full Game: All Courses + No Ads", and may later offer more courses as separate packs. Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details. Apple tells the game only what has been bought, so it can open those courses, turn off ads and restore them on your other devices.

The game's Cheat Code screen accepts promotional codes for the Full Game. The code is checked and redeemed by Apple, through the App Store. We do not learn who redeemed a code.

Apple's handling of purchases and codes is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

Now and then, after a race, the game may ask iOS to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple shows and handles it, and decides how often it appears. Any rating or review you leave goes to the App Store, not to us.

Vibration and notifications

The game uses vibration (haptics) for feedback. If you allow notifications, it can remind you about races with local notifications that your iPhone schedules itself; nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and you can turn them off any time in Settings. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Children's privacy

TurboTide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we collect none from anyone ourselves. The optional ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. A parent who wants no ads at all can buy the Full Game, or turn off in-app purchases and the game's network access in Screen Time.

Your privacy rights

Laws such as the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) give people rights to access, correct, delete or move their personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We respect these rights. We do not collect, store or receive any personal data ourselves, so we hold nothing about you to access, correct, delete or move, and we sell nothing. Information sent to Google for ads is held by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for your choices there, including resetting this app's identifier by deleting and reinstalling the game. To erase everything the game has saved, delete the app from your iPhone. Game Center data is held by Apple; manage it in your iPhone's Settings or at privacy.apple.com.

If you have a question or want to confirm this, email us (see below). We will answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. If a future version ever collects data, this page will say so before that version is released.
